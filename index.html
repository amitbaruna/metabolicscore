The Framework

5 Layers of Metabolic Permission™

Each layer feeds the next. When one breaks, it cascades. Fix the right one first.

LAYER 1

Circadian Authority

Sleep & recovery

Your sleep architecture, circadian rhythm, and recovery capacity. The foundation — every other layer depends on it.

LAYER 2

Neurochemical Safety

Stress & cortisol

Your nervous system's sense of safety. Chronic stress keeps cortisol elevated, breaks down muscle, stores belly fat.

LAYER 3

My Philosophy

"Helping people understand why their body responds the way it does — so they can change it with confidence."

Every symptom is a signal. What looks like resistance, fatigue, cravings, or weight regain is often the body's attempt to protect itself under chronic stress, poor recovery, or physiological overload.

Biology first, behaviour second. Sustainable habits don't come from motivation — they come from a nervous system that feels safe enough to change. Fix the biology and the behaviour follows.

The right intervention is rarely what you think. It's almost never the diet. It's almost never the workout. It's whatever is upstream of everything else — and finding that is the work.
